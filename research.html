Research

My work develops Bayesian methodology for structured data, especially spatial and graph-indexed problems. I am particularly interested in models whose probabilistic or geometric structure reflects the scientific object being analyzed rather than treating observations as generic Euclidean vectors.

Ongoing research directions

Generalized Bayes for complex-object data

Generalized Bayesian learning based on geometry-matched losses and divergences for nonstandard responses such as probability distributions, functions, and symmetric positive-definite matrices, with an emphasis on consistency and interpretable uncertainty quantification.

Spatial clustering and graph partitions

Bayesian random-partition models, graph-aware losses and distances, and posterior summarization for clustering problems in which spatial contiguity or network topology is part of the inferential target.

Complex-object regression and spatial processes

Regression and spatial process models for data living in non-Euclidean or constrained spaces, including methods that explicitly use Bregman geometry and local spatial structure.

Spatial causal inference

Developing methodology at the interface of Bayesian spatial modeling, uncertainty quantification, and causal inference for spatially structured observational data.
