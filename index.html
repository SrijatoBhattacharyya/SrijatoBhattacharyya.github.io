Welcome!

I am a Ph.D. student in the Department of Statistics at Texas A&M University, where I am advised by Prof. Huiyan Sang.

My research is centered on Bayesian methodology for structured and complex data, with particular interests in spatial statistics, random partitions and clustering, Bayesian nonparametrics, graph-based statistical methods, and uncertainty quantification. A recurring theme in my work is to develop models and inferential tools that respect the geometry or structural constraints of the data.

Before joining Texas A&M, I received an M.Stat. from the Indian Statistical Institute and a B.Sc. in Statistics from Presidency University, Kolkata.

Research interests

  • Bayesian spatial statistics
  • Bayesian nonparametrics
  • Random partitions and clustering
  • Graph-structured statistical methods
  • Generalized Bayes for complex objects
  • Uncertainty quantification
  • Statistical methodology for distributions, functions, and SPD matrices
  • Spatial causal inference
Current direction. I am developing Bayesian and generalized-Bayesian methodology for spatially structured complex-object data, including clustering, regression, and uncertainty quantification.
