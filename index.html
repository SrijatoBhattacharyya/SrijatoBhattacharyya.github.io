Welcome!

I am a Ph.D. student in the Department of Statistics at Texas A&M University, where I am advised by Prof. Huiyan Sang and Prof. Bani Mallick.

My research is centered on Bayesian methodology for structured and complex data, with particular interests in spatial statistics, random partitions and clustering, graph-based statistical methods, and uncertainty quantification. A recurring theme in my work is to develop models and inferential tools that respect the geometry or structural constraints of the data.

Before joining Texas A&M, I received an M.Stat. from the Indian Statistical Institute and a B.Sc. in Statistics from Presidency University, Kolkata.

Research interests

  • Bayesian spatial statistics
  • Bayesian nonparametrics
  • Random partitions and clustering
  • Graph-structured statistical methods
  • Generalized Bayes for complex objects
  • Uncertainty quantification
